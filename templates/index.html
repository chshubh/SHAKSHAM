How it works

  1. Tell us about you

    Five short steps: profile, goal, current ability and a safety check.

  2. Safety check first

    If your answers suggest exercise needs professional advice, we pause and say so.

  3. Get your routine

    A warm-up, main moves and cool-down that fit your time and equipment.

  4. Move and give feedback

    Guided timers, easy pause and stop, and a "How did that feel?" check after every exercise.

Every ability welcome

Seated, wall-supported and standing options. Exercises are filtered by what you tell us you find difficult.

Transparent by design

Simple, readable rules choose your exercises and we show you why each one was picked. No black box.

Consistency over intensity

Progress is about showing up and feeling confident, not about perfect bodies or punishing streaks.
